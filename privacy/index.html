Privacy

the short version

  • no ads, no cookies from vibegif, nothing sold or handed to anyone outside the services named here.
  • visits to vibegif's pages are counted and logged without IP addresses, and the log is deleted after 90 days.
  • hotlinking is not counted or logged: when an animation shows up on another site, we record nothing.
  • the only IP address we keep is a newsletter one: where you signed up from, or unsubscribed from if you did.
  • if you sign up for the newsletter your email address is kept until you ask for it to be deleted.

when you visit a page

visits are counted to know how many people use vibegif and to keep bots out of the numbers. a visit records the page, the page you came from, the time, and what your browser says about itself: its name and version, your system, language, time zone, screen size and whether it is a touch screen.

two counters see a visit. GoatCounter, a privacy-focused counting service, uses your IP address to tell visits apart and to work out your country, and does not store it. c, a small counter on our own server at c.vibegif.direct-img.link, keeps a log of visits with everything listed above for 90 days, then deletes it. c does not write your IP address into that log. it adds a visitor code instead, made from your IP address and browser with a key that is thrown away every day, so it can tell visitors apart within a day but can't be turned back into your address.

if your browser blocks GoatCounter, c passes your visit on to GoatCounter, IP address included, so GoatCounter can tell visits apart. an ad blocker therefore does not stop the count. this button does, for this browser:

the choice is saved in your browser, so it has to be made again on another device or after clearing this site's data.

animations on other sites

when another site shows a vibegif animation, your browser fetches it straight from our host like any image. vibegif doesn't count or log those, and the site showing it has its own privacy policy.

what stays in your browser

two settings, saved in your browser and never sent to us: whether you picked the black background, and the choice above if you make it.

others who see your request

vibegif is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles every request and so sees your IP address, as any host does. Cloudflare also shows us visit totals through Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies. the font loads from jsDelivr and the sponsor button on the gallery from GitHub. each of them sees your IP address and has its own privacy policy.

newsletter

if you sign up, your email address, the date and the IP address you signed up from are stored in a database at Cloudflare. a Cloudflare check called Turnstile runs on the form to make sure a person is signing up. issues are sent through Resend, which gets your email address in order to deliver them.

every issue has an unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe here. after that nothing more is sent, but your address stays on file marked as unsubscribed, with the date and the IP address used, until you ask for it to be deleted.

your data

write to vibegif@planetrenox.com to ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted. the visit log has no IP addresses or names in it, so we can't find your visits there.

the count and the log rest on our legitimate interest in knowing how vibegif is used and keeping it free of abuse, and the newsletter on your consent. if you are in the EU or the UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.
