should I use webp or gif?

the maintainers of vibegif recommend webp. it's actually more quality than gif, because gif is limited in color range (256 colors per frame) and semi-transparency (every pixel is either fully see-through or fully solid), so gradients get banded and soft edges get jagged. every animation here comes in both, so use the .gif only where webp isn't supported.
